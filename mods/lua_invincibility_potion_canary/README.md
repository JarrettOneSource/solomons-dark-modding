# Invincibility Potion

Adds a bright-green potion that can drop from defeated enemies. Ordinary
enemies have a 50 percent chance to drop one, while bosses always do.

The bottle has its own green artwork in the world and in your inventory.
Drinking it surrounds your character with a green glow, refills your mana, and
grants three minutes of invincibility and unlimited mana. Other players see the
glow on the character who drank it, and the host enforces the protection for
everyone in multiplayer. The same package runs in the rebuilt Website game and
the native Mod Loader.
